## 🌌 The Vision Behind Aurora-Lens

Imagine wearing a pair of spectacles that don't just correct your vision—they *augment* it. They let you perceive the subtle rhythms of the world around you, the hidden energy flows, the unspoken rules of physics, and the quiet potential of every object you encounter.

**Aurora-Lens-V2** is that spectacles for your digital expeditions. This is a meticulously crafted perception suite designed to recalibrate the fundamental parameters of your interactive experience. Built on the philosophy of **user-empowerment**, this suite is for the explorer who wants to write their own rules, the tinkerer who wonders "what if," and the connoisseur who appreciates the finer, often hidden, details of their favorite digital playgrounds.

## ✨ Core Enhancements & Features

This toolset is organized into four distinct "lenses," each focusing on a different aspect of your perception.

### 🏃 **Kinetic Lens: The Art of Effortless Motion**
This lens redefines your relationship with momentum and fatigue.
- **Endurance Override:** The invisible meter that usually dictates your resting periods is recalibrated. Run, sprint, and traverse without ever hitting the invisible wall of exhaustion. Your journey becomes a continuous, fluid motion.
- **Velocity Modulation:** Adjust your base movement speed to a comfortable glide or a thrilling rush. The choice is yours, tuned precisely to your preference.
- **Gravity Defiance (or Re-engagement):** Modify the vertical axis of your interaction. Leap to vantage points previously unreachable, or keep your feet firmly planted with a lower, more grounded jump profile.
- **Climbing Freedom:** Scale the world's geometry without the arbitrary limitation of a stamina gauge. Every handhold is a permanent rest point.

### 🔥 **Lumen Lens: The Light That Never Dies**
This suite focuses on your sources of illumination and utility.
- **Perpetual Radiance:** Your primary light source—be it a magical flame or a mechanical torch—burns with an inexhaustible glow. The darkness becomes a mere aesthetic, not an obstacle.
- **Utility Consumption Neutralization:** Tools and items that would normally be consumed or require rare resources to operate now function with a self-sustaining principle. Your focus remains on the task, not on resource management.

### 🛡️ **Cortex Lens: The Mind's Fortress**
Guard your perception from external interference.
- **Status Purification:** Filter out the temporary debuffs and environmental ailments that cloud your normal experience. Poison, confusion, slow, and other status interference are subsequently ignored, allowing you to perceive the world with pristine clarity.
- **Environmental Immunity:** Walk through hazardous zones with impunity. The negative feedback loop from the environment is silenced, letting you explore areas that are otherwise off-limits to the cautious observer.

### 👁️ **Observer's Toolkit: The Fine-Tuning Instruments**
- **Dynamic Parameter Adjustment:** A live, in-session interface that allows you to tweak the intensity of each lens on the fly. No need to restart; just adjust and continue.
- **Preset Configuration System:** Save your favorite perception profiles. Whether you're in "Tourist Mode" (extended views, zero fatigue) or "Adept Mode" (subtle tweaks), load your setup instantly.

## 🛠️ Installation & Setup

*We understand a seamless setup is key to a great experience.*

1.  **Download the Suite:** Acquire the latest release package from the repository's release section. Ensure you select the version matching your primary application's build.
2.  **Prepare the Fusion Layer:** Ensure your BepInEx framework is correctly installed in your application's root directory. This is the foundation that allows Aurora-Lens to communicate effectively.
3.  **Place the Core Module:** Extract the contents of the downloaded archive. Place the core plugin file (`AuroraLens_Core.dll`) into the `BepInEx/plugins` folder.
4.  **First Launch:** Start your primary application. Aurora-Lens will generate a default configuration file on its first run.
5.  **Configure Your Lenses:** Open the configuration file with a standard text editor (or use the in-game hotkey if enabled) to adjust the default parameters to your liking.

**Note:** The suite is designed to function as an overlay. It does not modify core game files, ensuring a high degree of stability and compatibility.
